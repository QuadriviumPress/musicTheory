# Open Music Theory

**Open Music Theory** is an open-source, interactive textbook for
college-level music theory courses. It supports active music making with
concise explanations, reference material, score examples, audio, video, and
interactive notation exercises.

```{image} images/open-books-on-path.jpg
:alt: Open books arranged along a wooded path
:class: omt-hero
```

The book was created from resources authored by Kris Shaffer, Bryn Hughes, and
Brian Moseley, and edited by Kris Shaffer and Robin Wharton. Major funding and
technical support for its interactive notation modules came from
[Trinket](https://trinket.io/).
