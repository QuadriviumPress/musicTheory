## Primary text

Shaffer, Bryn Hughes, and Brian Moseley and edited by Kris Shaffer and Robin
Wharton. The source edition was published by Hybrid Pedagogy Publishing and is
archived at the
[openmusictheory/openmusictheory.github.io repository](https://github.com/openmusictheory/openmusictheory.github.io).

## MyST edition

The Jekyll source was migrated to MyST Markdown for QuadriviumPress. Page
frontmatter, internal links, and asset paths were adapted to the MyST book
structure; the prose and educational sequence remain those of the source
edition.

The site scaffold derives from the
[Opinionated MyST Markdown Book Template](https://github.com/QuadriviumPress/opiniatedMystmdBookTemplate),
whose code is licensed under the MIT License.
